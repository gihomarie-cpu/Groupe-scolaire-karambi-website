Our History

Groupe Scolaire Saint Dominique Savio de Karambi — commonly known as G.S. Karambi — is a school with a long history in Rwanda’s education system. It is located in Karambi Sector, Nyamasheke District, in the Western Province of Rwanda.

The school has its origins in Catholic education and currently operates with government support. Throughout its long history, G.S. Karambi has played an important role in providing education and nurturing children and young people, preparing them to contribute to the development of the country.

  • 1931 — Establishment G.S. Karambi was established when education in Rwanda was still in its early stages and schools were few. It helped provide educational opportunities to children from Karambi and surrounding communities.
  • Growth over the decades The school gradually expanded the levels of education it offers, admitted more students, and increased its teachers and teaching and learning resources.
  • Government partnership G.S. Karambi began operating in partnership with the Government, enabling it to continue providing education to many students. It is listed by the National Institute of Statistics of Rwanda and by NESA as a Government-Aided school.
  • May 2017 — International Partnership G.S. Karambi formed a partnership with Edith-Stein-Gymnasium Speyer in Germany, connecting students and educators, sharing experiences, and supporting infrastructure and student well-being projects.
  • Today More than 90 years after its establishment, the school provides education from nursery and primary levels through secondary education, including Ordinary and approved Advanced Level combinations.

International Partnerships

One of the notable features of the recent history of G.S. Karambi is its partnership with a school outside Rwanda. Since May 2017, G.S. Karambi has had a partnership with Edith-Stein-Gymnasium Speyer in Germany.

This partnership has helped connect students and educators from the two countries, share experiences, and support activities aimed at improving education. It has also focused on projects related to infrastructure and students’ well-being, including the construction of rainwater collection and storage tanks that reduced water shortage s and improved hygiene at the school.

Recognition & Community

G.S. Karambi appears in records of education authorities and other government institutions. The National Institute of Statistics of Rwanda lists G.S. Karambi in Karambi Sector, Nyamasheke District, and NESA lists G.S. Saint Dominique Savio de Karambi as a Government-Aided school. This demonstrates the school’s recognised position within Rwanda’s education system.
